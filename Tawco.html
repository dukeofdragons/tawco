About Tawheed

A project support service company that carries out technical and commercial work in Kuwait, with a team that listens to what the customer needs and helps them get it done.

Tawheed is expanding its operations with an expert, highly qualified team that understands customer requirements and helps clients meet them without hindrance.

The company has earned its standing in the industry through the support of its employees. Experienced people are recruited only after their skills have been assessed against the specification of the job.

Core values

  • We treat our clients with respect and faith.
  • We grow through creativity, technology and quality solutions.
  • We bring honesty, integrity and business ethics into every part of how we work.

Goals

  • Expand regionally in industrial services and manpower solutions, with a strong base of key customers.
  • Grow the company's assets and investments to support industrial services.
  • Build a good reputation in industrial project support and become a key player in the industry.

Contracting and trading

Tawheed carries out general civil engineering construction. Qualified engineers and supervisors with long experience in civil construction lead the work, backed by a committed workforce.

We keep a range of heavy equipment and transport on hand so the work reaches the best result at a fair cost, with no compromise on quality.

We work to reduce our environmental impact and keep improving our environmental performance as part of how we operate, and we encourage our customers, suppliers and contractors to do the same.
